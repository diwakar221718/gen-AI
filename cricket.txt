On fields of green where dreams take flight,
A bat meets ball in golden light.
The cheers arise, the hearts all race,
As heroes shine with skill and grace.

A bowler storms with fiery pace,
A batter stands with fearless face.
The ball takes flight beyond the rope,
A soaring six ignites new hope.

The wicket falls, the crowd grows still,
Yet every player shows strong will.
Through every run and every dive,
The game keeps passion all alive.

From dusty lanes to stadium bright,
Cricket unites by day and night.
It teaches courage, trust, and pride,
With teammates always side by side.

So raise the bat, let victory sing,
For every match new dreams can bring.
More than a sport, it's joy untold—
A timeless story, brave and bold.
